SIMS (Sudharm Infotech Management System) is a complete offline ERP developed for Sudharm Infotech (MKCL Authorized Learning Center).

The software manages the complete institute workflow from inquiry to admission, payments, reports, certificates and administration.

The application is designed to eliminate manual work while keeping the workflow extremely fast for daily office operations.

The application is optimized for desktop use.

Primary users are institute administrators and office staff.
